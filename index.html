How it works

DIR means Day In Room. The engagement is one structured working session, run in a single day, with your leadership team in the room. The answer is built and agreed there. What comes before is preparation; what comes after is documentation and a window of field-testing support.

1

Fit Check

You answer a short guided set of questions so we can determine whether this is actually a positioning problem and whether your team is ready.

2

Input Review

If it looks promising, you submit your current homepage or pitch, competitors, customer examples, and one sales call if available.

3

The Room, First Half

Who you are really for, the alternatives you are measured against, and the points where your current story breaks. Worked in the room until the team agrees.

4

The Room, Second Half

What you can claim, the proof behind each claim, and the objections the claim has to survive. The unique value proposition takes its final shape here.

5

The Agreed Answer

The deliverable is what the room decided: one unique value proposition your leadership has tested and signed off on, then documented. It is not a recap of the day and not a set of options for you to choose from later.

6

Field-Testing Support

You leave with a way to pressure-test the answer in-market over an agreed window. If real-world results call for refinement, we support the changes that documentation and proof require, at no additional charge.

What happens after the sprint?

Usually, the answer is simple: your internal team, agency, fractional executive, or sales consultant uses the output. That is the point. This sprint is designed to make the people already responsible for execution more effective, not to replace them.

  • A documented unique value proposition
  • Aligned leadership language
  • Message boundaries and proof standards
  • A usable reference point for web, sales, and marketing review

We do not sell marketing retainers, ad management, sales consulting, or broad implementation services. The engagement produces one outcome — a unique value proposition your leadership has agreed on — and stops there. After the session you have an agreed window to field-test the answer. If what you learn calls for refinement, we support the documentation and proof changes that follow, at no additional charge. A finding that would change the answer itself is a different conclusion, and that belongs back in the room as a separate engagement, not a free redo.
