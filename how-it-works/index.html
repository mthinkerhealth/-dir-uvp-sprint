How it works

How the session actually works

The method behind a UVP Sprint: how capable teams turn the shared answer to why customers buy into a unique value proposition the organization can use.

  • The premise

    It is easy to get strategically ahead of yourself.

    A company can have strategy work, brand work, sales enablement, a new website, and a leadership offsite behind it, and still not have a shared answer to the question that guides the rest of the work: why do customers buy from us? The unique value proposition is that answer made explicit, tested, and usable.

  • September 8, 2026

    Nobody Can Referee Their Own Argument

    Most leadership alignment work fails for a structural reason, not a quality reason. The person facilitating may have something else to sell, or may work for someone whose claims need to be tested.
  • September 15, 2026

    Four Rules Before the Session Starts

    A UVP Sprint session uses four fixed roles and one repeating method for testing every claim a leadership team makes about itself.
  • September 22, 2026

    Same Questions, Different Answers

    A UVP Sprint uses the same pressure in each session so each leadership team can reach its own tested answer to why customers buy.
  • September 29, 2026

    Consensus Is Not Language

    Why the deliverable is agreement rather than wording, and why a leader who stayed quiet during the session keeps the power to undo the result months later.
  • October 6, 2026

    Why We Turn Away Deals

    Why the people who hold the decisions have to be present, why a delegate cannot stand in for them, and why the cost of that rule is accepted rather than regretted.
